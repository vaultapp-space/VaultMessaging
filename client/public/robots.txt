# https://vaultapp.space/robots.txt

# The default posture is open: this is a marketing site and everything under /
# that renders is meant to be found.
User-agent: *
Allow: /
# The API answers 401 to anonymous requests, so crawling it produces nothing
# but error pages that dilute the crawl budget for the pages that matter.
Disallow: /api/
# Uploaded post and story media. Public by URL, but a UUID filename is not
# content anyone searches for, and these are deleted after 24 hours — indexing
# them manufactures a permanent supply of 404s.
Disallow: /uploads/
# The self-hosted F-Droid repository: package indexes and APK binaries.
Disallow: /fdroid/

# AI crawlers — GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended — are allowed
# deliberately, and are covered by the group above rather than by groups of
# their own. That is not laziness: a crawler obeys exactly one group, the most
# specific one matching its name, and the groups do not merge. Naming GPTBot
# and granting it "Allow: /" would therefore have *removed* the three Disallow
# rules above for it, which is the opposite of the intent.
#
# The intent itself: Vault's case rests on specifics — no phone number, 24-hour
# expiry, X3DH and the Double Ratchet — and answer engines are increasingly
# where someone first asks which messenger doesn't need a phone number. Being
# absent from that answer is a real cost, and nothing here is worth withholding.
#
# To exclude one of them later, copy the three Disallow lines into its group.
